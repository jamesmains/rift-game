# 1.5.0 (#96): Field Notes - what the party learns about a creature (hit with a damage type, spells it casts, three
# slain = everything) goes into fieldnotes.ini in the save folder, for every slot. After a wipe and a reload the tooltip
# (knownResBits) and the bestiary still know it. A fresh save folder starts empty. (1.6.2 #137: the launcher's Field
# Notes page was removed; the notes still feed the tooltip and the Journal's bestiary.)
# Expect keys: fieldnote:ID ("res bits;tags,"), fieldknown:ID (what the tooltip shows), bestiaryids.
frames 5
newgame Kai mage 1 20261116
god
frames 30
expect fieldnote:skeleton == none
expect bestiaryids lacks skeleton
saveslot 1
frames 5
teleport hermit_hut 4 14
frames 120
cb arena 2 11 21 7
combat skeleton,necromancer
frames 2
cb forcehit 1
unitplace 0 4 14
unitplace 1 6 14
unitplace 2 8 16
cb turn 0
cb hp 1 500
cb hp 2 500
# a blade (physical, bit 1) and a fire spell (fire, bit 2) on the skeleton
cb attack 0 1
expect fieldnote:skeleton contains 1;
cb cast 0 fireball 6 14
expect fieldknown:skeleton == 3
expect savefile:fieldnotes.ini == 1
# the necromancer casts a dark bolt: what it casts goes into the notes too
cb cast 2 dark_bolt 4 14
expect fieldnote:necromancer contains spell:dark_bolt
# ---- the party falls; the reload knows nothing of this fight in the save, but the notes remain ----
partywipe

expect mode == EXPLORE
expect fieldknown:skeleton == 3
expect bestiaryids contains skeleton
