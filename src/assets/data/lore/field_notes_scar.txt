Field notes - the Scar, three visits.

1. The ground glows faintly at night. Not radiation (the Geiger stick from the
   lab says nothing). More like it remembers being lit.
2. Creatures near the Scar are wrong in small ways: too many joints, eyes that
   track you through walls. The hunters call them riftspawn. They do not bleed
   the right colour.
3. There is a structure at the centre that was not there on the second visit.
   I did not go in. I am not a brave man, only a curious one.

If someone braver reads this: bring light. Bring a lot of light.
