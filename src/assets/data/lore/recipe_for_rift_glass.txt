Rift glass - Hald's notes (do not tell the Arcanist)

Take three measures of ordinary quartz, one of the blue dust that gathers
on the Scar's edge, and heat them in the forge until the mix sings. It will
sing. Pull it the moment the note changes.

Rift glass holds a charge like nothing else: a blade edged with it burns, a
lens of it lets you see what the dark is hiding. Every smith in Brasswick
wants the recipe. Every smith in Brasswick can keep wanting.

Materials wanted: quartz, blue dust (the Scar), iron for the fittings.
Bring them to the workbench and we'll talk.
