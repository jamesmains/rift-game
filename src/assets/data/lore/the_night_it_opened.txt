Forty years ago, on the night of the first frost, the sky over Aldmoor tore.

Everyone who saw it describes it the same way: not a light, a seam. Something
behind the stars was pressing through, and for one night the land underneath
belonged to no single world. The glass towers of Brasswick's "new quarter"
were already standing in the morning, with people still asleep inside them who
had never heard of Aldmoor. The ship above the Scar came down burning around
noon. The machines in the deep labs woke up a week later, which is how we know
they had been there all along, waiting under our feet.

The Rift closed by itself. Nobody closed it. Remember that when the priests
tell you otherwise.
