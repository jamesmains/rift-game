# Factions: the towns and groups the party has a reputation with (-100 to 100, starts at 0).
# Dialogue/scripts change it with rep=FACTION:N (rep=hollowmere:5,brasswick:-3); conditions test it with rep:FACTION:N
# (true when reputation is at least N; !rep:FACTION:N for "below N"). Achievements use rep:FACTION>=N.
# A shop with faction=ID prices by it: up to 15% off when Honoured, up to 25% more when Hated.
# Paying a bounty at a notice board adds the board's rep= (default 3) to the faction with the board's id.
# Tiers: Hated <= -50, Disliked <= -10, Neutral, Liked >= 10, Friendly >= 30, Honoured >= 60.
# A faction id without an entry here still works; it shows the board name or the id.
# 1.1.2 - what standing is good for, and what it costs (the notice board's Standing page, Tab):
#   reward=REP|item:n,gold:n|title           a favour, claimed once at the town's notice board when standing reaches REP
#   project=title|item:n,...|rep|gold|text   civic projects, delivered one after another at the board (+rep, gold, XP)
#   Bounties raise standing only up to 30 (Friendly); past that it takes projects and story choices. Dropping a taken
#   notice costs 2. Disliked (-10): the board posts no work. Hated (-50): the town's shops won't trade. Below 0 the
#   board offers to make amends for gold (back to 0, never higher). A shop's item=id:stock:REP only shows from REP up.
