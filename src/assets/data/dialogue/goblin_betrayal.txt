# 1.5.0 (#85): after sparing Grakk, the tribe packs up in the chief's hall (goblins_packing, until the party next enters
# Greywood Cave). Walking back into the hall (trigger) or talking to Grakk or a packer offers the betrayal - only through
# an explicit prompt. Attacking sets goblins_betrayed, clears goblin_chief_spared (so his groups show again) and fights
# the chief's group (fight=goblin_chief_dead). Orin's red line (#88): he won't stand for killing people who surrendered.
[start]
speaker=$hero
text=Grakk's hall is all noise and torchlight: pots, bedrolls, spears bundled with string, the old ones being lifted onto backs. Grakk sees you and goes very still. "Tall-folk. Grakk gave you the crown. Grakk is going. Is there more?"
option=No. Go.|end
option=(Attack them anyway.)|confirm|goblin_chief_spared&!goblins_allies

[confirm]
speaker=$hero
text=They surrendered to you, and they are leaving. If you draw now, every goblin in this hall fights for its life - and everyone who hears of it will know you broke your word. There is no taking it back.
option=(Put your weapon away.)|end
option=(Attack them anyway.)|attack|!party:cleric
option=(Attack them anyway.)|orin|party:cleric

# Orin's red line: killing people who surrendered
[orin]
speaker=Orin
text=(Orin steps in front of you, mace lowered.) No. They SURRENDERED, $hero. They are carrying their grandmothers. If you do this, you do it without me. I will not stand in that hall and call it justice.
option=(Put your weapon away.) You're right.|orin_relief
option=They raided three farms and shot a boy. A promise made to save my own neck isn't a vow, Orin.|orin_cha|stat:cha>=13
option=That crown sang to them for a year. Their young know nothing but raiding. They'll be back by spring.|orin_wis|stat:wis>=13
